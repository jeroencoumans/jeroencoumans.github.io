About Resume

I'm a maker at heart. Ever since I was a child, I've loved getting lost in the creative process. What started with drawing and painting eventually became a career spanning 20+ years at the intersection of design and engineering.

I've worked on products used by millions, co-founded two companies and built and led product and engineering teams. But what gives me the most energy is still designing and building things myself. I enjoy turning complex problems into simple, functional and beautiful user experiences.

I'm increasingly driven by what I build, not just how I build it. Now I want to build products that make a positive impact beyond the screen, ideally contributing to a more sustainable future.
